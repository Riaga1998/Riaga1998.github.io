01
AI/ML · Bioinformatics · Live Demo
Genome Firewall Live Demo ↗
Hack-Nation Global AI Hackathon, Jul 2026 · team selected from 5,500 applications across 125 countries

An ML system that predicts which antibiotics will fail against an infection from an assembled bacterial genome, before lab results come back one to three days later. I built the data and genome-to-features pipeline: AMRFinderPlus encodes resistance genes and point mutations as interpretable binary features, training data is filtered down to lab-verified labels only, and evaluation uses MinHash/Jaccard cluster-level splitting so near-identical genomes can't leak across the split. Designed around honesty: calibrated confidence, a no-call option when evidence is weak, and a rule that every failure prediction has to cite a detected determinant.

Brassica miRNA Evolution PDF ↗
Research internship · JNU

A synteny-based comparative genomics study tracing how the MIR156 and MIR172 microRNA families evolved across the five Brassica species of the U's triangle. Ran the full pipeline in BLAST and MEGA7: identified 177 MIR156 and 56 MIR172 orthologs, mapped their chromosomal locations, and built maximum-likelihood phylogenies that placed MIR156i as the most recently evolved family member. 05About & My Why
Why AI and health · how I like to work

Every project here traces back to one thing that happened before I could remember it.

I've wanted to use science and technology to solve problems that matter since I was ten, when I read about a cancer vaccine being developed abroad. A personal loss made it matter, and it's been my north star ever since.

The journey wasn't linear, but it led somewhere clear: today I build and evaluate AI products, driven by the belief that the right technology can genuinely help people. What I care about most is trust.

In anything that affects real people, a confident wrong answer is worse than an honest "I don't know."

I'd rather ship something reliable than something that just looks good on a slide.
