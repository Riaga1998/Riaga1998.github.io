~/story

Curiosity started it. It keeps me going.

At ten, I read about a cancer vaccine being developed abroad and couldn't stop wondering how something like that gets made. Curiosity took me from biology to computer science, my way back into medicine, then to Parkinson's research and a patent application, and into technical support, where I barely knew what a localhost was, learned where real users get stuck, and turned those pain points into shipped fixes. Today it has me building AI products at Wharton and doing clinical research, where my pathology poster just won Best Research Poster at SIIM. Still loving the journey, and curious about what’s ahead.
