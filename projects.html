AI/ML · Bioinformatics · Live Demo
Genome Firewall Live Demo ↗
Hack-Nation Global AI Hackathon, Jul 2026 · team selected from 5,500 applications across 125 countries

An ML system that predicts which antibiotics will fail against an infection from an assembled bacterial genome, before lab results come back one to three days later. I built the data and genome-to-features pipeline: AMRFinderPlus encodes resistance genes and point mutations as interpretable binary features, training data is filtered down to lab-verified labels only, and evaluation uses MinHash/Jaccard cluster-level splitting so near-identical genomes can't leak across the split. Designed around honesty: calibrated confidence, a no-call option when evidence is weak, and a rule that every failure prediction has to cite a detected determinant.

Instalily case study

A Claude tool-use agent for appliance-parts e-commerce, scoped to refrigerators and dishwashers. It diagnoses before it sells: clarifying questions and free checks first, then the likely part with the reason it could be the culprit. Part numbers, prices and compatibility come only from six tools, never from the model's memory, and compatibility returns a clear yes, no or unknown verdict. 05
AI Evaluation · LLM-as-Judge · Research
Brand Guideline Scoring Harness
Active · Wharton, advised by Prof. Kartik Hosanagar

A Python harness that scores AI-written marketing posts against a company's brand guidelines. Rules that can be counted, like exclamation marks, sentence case and reading level, run as code. Tone and judgment calls go to a Claude judge that has to quote the post as evidence, and code checks the quote is really in the post. When the judge missed a claim the brand forbids, I had code pull out every sentence that could break the rule and added a severity cap so one clear violation limits the score. Now extending it to work for any brand: it drafts a rubric from a brand's documents, with every rule tied to a quote from the source and a person approving it before use.

Research · Data Engineering
Histology Image Compression
Best Research Poster, 1st Place · SIIM CAIMI26

Tile-based pipelines for multi-gigapixel whole-slide TIFF images, and a study of who a compression setting is really measured for. Much of a pathology slide is blank background, so an average can hide what happens to the tissue. Tissue-aware distributional matching for JPEG 2000 gave a 1.7× storage reduction at JPEG-80-equivalent quality. Presented at SIIM CAIMI26 in Philadelphia, where it won 1st place.

Bioinformatics · Publication
Brassica miRNA Evolution PDF ↗
Research internship · JNU

A synteny-based comparative genomics study tracing how the MIR156 and MIR172 microRNA families evolved across the five Brassica species of the U's triangle. Ran the full pipeline in BLAST and MEGA7: identified 177 MIR156 and 56 MIR172 orthologs, mapped their chromosomal locations, and built maximum-likelihood phylogenies that placed MIR156i as the most recently evolved family member. First author, and the only intern on the paper.
